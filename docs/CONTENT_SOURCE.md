# Content Source — Fitness Sin Filtros

## Editorial voice

Spanish (Latin America). Warm, direct, disciplined, non-judgmental, professional, and motivating. Speak to a person, not an algorithm. Avoid all-or-nothing claims.

## Never publish

- “Baja de peso garantizado”.
- “Resultados en pocos días”.
- “Elimina toda la grasa”.
- “Dieta milagrosa”.
- “Cuerpo perfecto”.
- “Sin esfuerzo”.
- Any invented credential, client, review, outcome, or medical recommendation.
